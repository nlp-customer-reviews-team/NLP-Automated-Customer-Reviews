# Amazon Fire TV: A Solid Streaming Device with Minor Flaws
[The Amazon Fire TV stands out as a reliable streaming device, but it's not without its flaws.]

## What reviewers like
One of the standout features of the Amazon Fire TV is its seamless streaming experience. Nearly half of the 4- and 5-star reviews praise its ability to stream content smoothly, whether it’s movies, TV shows, or live sports. Additionally, users appreciate the device’s quick and efficient performance, which contributes to a smooth viewing experience. The user interface is also praised for being intuitive and easy to navigate, making it accessible for both tech-savvy individuals and those who prefer a more straightforward setup.

## Weak spots
However, there are some areas where the Fire TV falls short. Connectivity issues, particularly with Wi-Fi and Ethernet connections, are reported by nearly a quarter of the 1- and 2-star reviews. Users frequently complain about unstable internet connections, which can lead to buffering and poor quality of video playback. Another common complaint is the limited availability of apps, with 16% of negative reviews citing this issue. Setting up the device can also be challenging for some users, with 15% of negative reviews mentioning difficulties during the initial setup process.

## Bottom line
Overall, the Amazon Fire TV remains a solid choice for streaming enthusiasts, offering a reliable and enjoyable experience. However, potential buyers should be aware of the connectivity and app availability issues, which can detract from the overall satisfaction. For those willing to overlook these minor drawbacks, the Fire TV continues to be a dependable option in the streaming device market.
